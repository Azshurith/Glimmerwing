Glimmerwing

How to win. Pop a dandelion puff and its pollen burst pops its neighbours. Chains of three or more send falling stars into your rival’s meadow; seven or more add a thorny bramble. Fill the gauge at the bottom to send your fairy’s guardian. You can also send one early: hold fire past a full charge to overcharge (costs half a gauge), or break four incoming stars within four seconds for a rally. Guardians drift in and shadow you, lock in place to attack, then sink out through the bottom.

Bat stars back. A star’s colour shows the hits it needs: rose takes four, orange three, gold two, pale one. Break it to send it back tougher, up to three times. The longer a round runs, the faster everything comes at you (watch the tempo in the corner), and each new round of a match starts faster. Lose all five hearts and you lose the round; best of three takes the match.

Fever. About half a minute in, “Moonseed rising” flashes and a pale crescent orb drifts in with the next puffs. Shots bounce off it; catch it in a pollen burst to break it. That starts Fever: a spread shot, a rush of puffs, a gauge that fills twice as fast, and every chain sends one star per pop after the first, so a 5-chain sends four.

Tough puffs and late rounds. A puff’s seed tips show the shots it takes, dropping a step with each hit: moonsilver five, amber four, sky three, mint two, cream one. Any pollen burst pops it outright. Some waves come wrapped in dew bubbles that pop first, big puffs leave extra-wide bursts, and watch for waves that sneak up from below. Beetles cut diagonals inside a glassy shell that has to break first; spinners corkscrew down. Waves also swoop in from a corner, sweep across in a line, or drop in squeezing columns, and some ring a tough leader with small fry: pop the leader to clear the lot. Tougher critters leave bigger bursts. Break a rose star and it flies back as an unbreakable comet you can only dodge. If a round runs past about a minute and a half, hollow crows wake in both meadows and start diving at you. Shoot one enough to drive it off, but it comes back faster.

Fairies. Three fairies are ready from the start. Win matches, chain big, and beat the CPU cleanly to unlock three more. Progress is saved in this browser.

Controls. Vs CPU: arrows or WASD, tap Space to shoot, hold to charge. On touch, drag to steer and hold to charge. Two players: WASD + F and arrows + Enter. P pauses.
